## Overview

A payload concept that brings long-wave infrared and short-wave infrared information together to help an operator interpret a scene.

## Technical approach

LWIR sensing highlights thermal contrast, while SWIR can reveal reflected-light detail and material differences that thermal imagery may not show. Presented together, the views can help an operator cross-check observations rather than relying on one sensor alone.
